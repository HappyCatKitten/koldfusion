File-by-file (URL / description / CF features / CF4.5 compatibility):

100_doors.cfm                  https://rosettacode.org/wiki/100_doors
  "Another Route" solution: toggles 100 doors in a comma list.
  Tags: CFPARAM CFLOOP(from/to/step) CFIF/CFELSE CFSET CFOUTPUT. Funcs: ListGetAt ListSetAt. CF4: yes. Mixed-case tag names (<Cfset>) - good parser test.

100_doors_cfscript_fragment.cfm https://rosettacode.org/wiki/100_doors
  "Basic Solution" CFScript body WITHOUT <cfscript> wrapper (verbatim). Needs wrapping in <cfscript> to run.
  Script: while loops, if/else. Funcs: ListAppend ListLen ListGetAt ListSetAt Int. CF4.5 cfscript: yes.

99_bottles.cfm                 https://rosettacode.org/wiki/99_bottles_of_beer
  Classic tag-based 99 Bottles. Tags: CFOUTPUT CFLOOP(step=-1) CFSET. Funcs: IIf DE. CF4: yes.
  (Loop goes to 0 - prints a "0 bottles" verse - that's in the original.)

fizzbuzz.cfm                   https://rosettacode.org/wiki/FizzBuzz
  Tags: CFLOOP CFIF/CFELSEIF/CFELSE CFOUTPUT; MOD operator. CF4: yes.
  (Original has Fizz/Buzz swapped for 3/5 - a known quirk of the entry.)

harshad_niven.cfm              https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Harshad_or_Niven_series
  First 20 Harshad numbers + first > 1000. Tags: CFLOOP(condition) CFIF CFSET CFOUTPUT. Funcs: Len Left Right; MOD. CF4: yes.

evolutionary_weasel.cfm        https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Evolutionary_algorithm
  Dawkins "METHINKS IT IS LIKE A WEASEL". Tags: CFLOOP(condition/from-to) CFPARAM(dynamic name "child#child#") CFIF CFFLUSH CFOUTPUT.
  Funcs: Mid RandRange Rand Len NumberFormat; variables["child" & n] struct-style scope access.
  CF4.5: NO as-is - CFFLUSH is CF5+, bracket access to VARIABLES scope is CF5/MX-era. Easy to adapt (Evaluate/SetVariable).

day_of_week.cfm                https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Day_of_the_week
  Years 2008-2121 where Dec 25 is a Sunday. Tags: CFLOOP CFSET CFIF CFOUTPUT. Funcs: CreateDate DayOfWeek. CF4: yes (uses XHTML-style "/>" closers).

substring.cfm                  https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Substring
  Classic tag version. Tags: CFOUTPUT CFSET; CFML comments. Funcs: Mid Right Left Len Find. CF4: yes.

greatest_element_loop.cfm      https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Greatest_element_of_a_list
  CFLOOP list= over '1, 1000, 250, 13'. Tags: CFSET CFPARAM CFLOOP(list) CFIF CFOUTPUT. CF4: yes.
greatest_element_listsort.cfm  (same page) Funcs: ListFirst ListSort(numeric,desc). CF4: yes.

strip_chars.cfm                https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Strip_a_set_of_characters_from_a_string
  Funcs: Replace(...,'all') Mid Len. Tags: CFLOOP CFSET CFOUTPUT. CF4: yes.

repeat_string.cfm              https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Repeat_a_string
  Tags: CFSET CFOUTPUT CFLOOP. CF4: yes. (RepeatString() would be the one-liner.)

loops_nested.cfm               https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Loops/Nested
  Random numbers until 20. Tags: CFLOOP(condition, from/to) CFBREAK CFIF CFOUTPUT. Funcs: RandRange. CF4: yes.

loops_break.cfm                https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Loops/Break
  Tags: CFLOOP(condition) CFBREAK CFIF CFOUTPUT. Funcs: RandRange. CF4: yes.

is_numeric.cfm                 https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Determine_if_a_string_is_numeric
  Tags: CFSET CFIF/CFELSE CFOUTPUT. Funcs: IsNumeric. CF4: yes.

walk_directory.cfm             https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Walk_a_directory/Non-recursively
  CFDIRECTORY action=list filter="*.html" + CFOUTPUT query=. Needs filesystem (C:\temp). CF4: yes.

file_io.cfm                    https://rosettacode.org/wiki/File_input/output
  CFFILE read/write. Funcs: FileExists ExpandPath. CF4: yes.

date_format.cfm                https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Date_format
  Funcs: DateFormat Now. CF4: yes.

cfswitch_example.cfm           https://rosettacode.org/wiki/Conditional_structures
  Skeleton CFSWITCH/CFCASE/CFDEFAULTCASE (expects variable x). CF4: yes.
