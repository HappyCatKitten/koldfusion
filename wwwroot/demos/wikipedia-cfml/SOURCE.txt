Description: The canonical minimal custom-tag example: addition.cfm sets caller.addition = attributes.first + attributes.second and exits;
             call_addition.cfm invokes it as <cf_addition first="1" second="2"> (then #addition# is available to the caller).
CF features: custom tags (CF_ prefix lookup in current dir / CustomTags), ATTRIBUTES and CALLER scopes, CFEXIT method="exitTag".
Datasource: none. CF4.5 compatible: yes. (call_addition.cfm is verbatim and does not itself output the result.)
