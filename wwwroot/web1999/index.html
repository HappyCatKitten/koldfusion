The 1999 Web Ring
Twelve sites from the dot-com boom, lovingly re-imagined and hosted on KoldFusion 4.5



1. Yowza!  (a takeoff of Yahoo!)
The web's favourite hand-built directory. Fourteen categories and counting.
2. AltaVisor  (a takeoff of AltaVista)
Smart searching. Our spider has read every page on the 1999 Web.
3. Ask Reeves  (a takeoff of Ask Jeeves)
Ask a question in plain English. Your butler will see to it.
4. Orinoco.com  (a takeoff of Amazon.com)
Earth's biggest bookstore, named after a different river.
5. uBid  (a takeoff of eBay)
Your personal trading community. Beanie Babies going fast!
6. eToyz  (a takeoff of eToys)
Toys delivered before Christmas. Probably.
7. Petz.com  (a takeoff of Pets.com)
Because pets can't drive! (Sold at a loss.)
8. GeoHoods  (a takeoff of GeoCities)
Free home pages! Hundreds of homesteaders in themed neighborhoods.
9. Hamster Shuffle  (a takeoff of Hampster Dance)
Rows and rows of dancing hamsters. Turn your speakers up.
10. Dashdot  (a takeoff of Slashdot)
News for nerds. Stuff that matters. First post!
11. Snapster  (a takeoff of Napster)
Share your music library. Legal department pending.
12. CNM Interactive  (a takeoff of CNN.com)
Breaking news, weather, and the Y2K countdown.
