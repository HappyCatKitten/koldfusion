How to write a Boolean expression
  • AND - both words must appear: hamster AND wheel
  • OR - either word may appear: cat OR kitten
  • AND NOT - leave out pages with the second word: java AND NOT coffee
  • NEAR - both words, close together (Skeeter is generous about "close"): bean NEAR bag
Words in the Ranking box do not change which pages are found, only the order they come back in. Dates are compared with the date each page was last modified.
