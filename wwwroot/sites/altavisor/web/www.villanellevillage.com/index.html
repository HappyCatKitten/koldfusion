*** Welcome to Villanelle Village *** Updated May 2, 1999 *** Sign my guestbook! ***

A whole neighborhood devoted to the villanelle. Yes, the whole thing.

Our monthly contest is open to anyone with a scanner or a very steady hand with the mouse. We add new pieces to the gallery every week, so bookmark us and come back often.

Here at Villanelle Village we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures. This page is dedicated to poetry and to everyone who has ever been told to get a real job.
