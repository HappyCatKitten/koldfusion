*** Welcome to Juggle This *** Updated June 8, 1999 *** Sign my guestbook! ***

Juggling patterns animated in Java, and a forum for dropped clubs.

The best way to look at the gallery is with your monitor set to thousands of colors. All works shown here are copyright their creators. Please ask before you borrow anything.

Here at Juggle This we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures. Our monthly contest is open to anyone with a scanner or a very steady hand with the mouse.
