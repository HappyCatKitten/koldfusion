Lighthouse Lovers Online

Photos and histories of lighthouses on every coast, with a keeper's diary.

This page is dedicated to architecture and to everyone who has ever been told to get a real job. The best way to look at the gallery is with your monitor set to thousands of colors.

Our monthly contest is open to anyone with a scanner or a very steady hand with the mouse. Here at Lighthouse Lovers Online we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures.
