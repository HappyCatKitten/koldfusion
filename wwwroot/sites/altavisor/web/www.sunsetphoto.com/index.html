Sunset Photo of the Day

Every day, one sunset. Thousands of them now. Still pretty.

Our monthly contest is open to anyone with a scanner or a very steady hand with the mouse. Here at Sunset Photo of the Day we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures.

This page is dedicated to photography and to everyone who has ever been told to get a real job. We add new pieces to the gallery every week, so bookmark us and come back often.
