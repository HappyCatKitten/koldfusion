Sidewalk Chalk Society

Last updated January 20, 1999

Photos of street chalk art before the rain.

Our monthly contest is open to anyone with a scanner or a very steady hand with the mouse. Here at Sidewalk Chalk Society we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures.

We add new pieces to the gallery every week, so bookmark us and come back often. All works shown here are copyright their creators. Please ask before you borrow anything.
