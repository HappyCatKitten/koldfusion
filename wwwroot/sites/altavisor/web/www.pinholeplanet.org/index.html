Pinhole Planet

Build a camera out of an oatmeal box and take pictures with it.

Our monthly contest is open to anyone with a scanner or a very steady hand with the mouse. We add new pieces to the gallery every week, so bookmark us and come back often.

All works shown here are copyright their creators. Please ask before you borrow anything. Here at Pinhole Planet we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures.
