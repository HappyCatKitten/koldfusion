Logo Laughs

A gallery of the worst corporate logos ever designed by a nephew.

The best way to look at the gallery is with your monitor set to thousands of colors. Here at Logo Laughs we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures.

Our monthly contest is open to anyone with a scanner or a very steady hand with the mouse. We add new pieces to the gallery every week, so bookmark us and come back often.
