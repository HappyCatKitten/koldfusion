Library of Lost Endings

Readers write new final chapters for famous unfinished novels.

Our monthly contest is open to anyone with a scanner or a very steady hand with the mouse. Here at Library of Lost Endings we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures.

All works shown here are copyright their creators. Please ask before you borrow anything. We add new pieces to the gallery every week, so bookmark us and come back often.
