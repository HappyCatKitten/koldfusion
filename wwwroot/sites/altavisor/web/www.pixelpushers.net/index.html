Pixel Pushers

16-color art made in paint programs, displayed with pride.

Here at Pixel Pushers we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures. We add new pieces to the gallery every week, so bookmark us and come back often.

Our monthly contest is open to anyone with a scanner or a very steady hand with the mouse. The best way to look at the gallery is with your monitor set to thousands of colors.
