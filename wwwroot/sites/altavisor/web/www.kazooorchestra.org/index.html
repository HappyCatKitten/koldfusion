The world's largest kazoo ensemble needs YOU.

We add new pieces to the gallery every week, so bookmark us and come back often. Here at The Kazoo Orchestra we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures.

Our monthly contest is open to anyone with a scanner or a very steady hand with the mouse. This page is dedicated to performing arts and to everyone who has ever been told to get a real job.
