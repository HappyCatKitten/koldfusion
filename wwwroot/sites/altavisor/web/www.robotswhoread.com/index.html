Robots Who Read

Last updated June 19, 1999

Reviews of science fiction novels written from the point of view of a toaster.

We add new pieces to the gallery every week, so bookmark us and come back often. Our monthly contest is open to anyone with a scanner or a very steady hand with the mouse.

The best way to look at the gallery is with your monitor set to thousands of colors. Here at Robots Who Read we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures.


Best viewed with Netscape Navigator at 800x600
