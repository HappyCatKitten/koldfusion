Couplets for Cats

*** Welcome to Couplets for Cats *** Updated October 18, 1999 *** Sign my guestbook! ***

Poems written for, about and occasionally by cats.

Here at Couplets for Cats we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures. We add new pieces to the gallery every week, so bookmark us and come back often.

Our monthly contest is open to anyone with a scanner or a very steady hand with the mouse. All works shown here are copyright their creators. Please ask before you borrow anything.
