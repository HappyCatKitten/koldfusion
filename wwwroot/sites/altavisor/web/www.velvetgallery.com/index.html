The finest paintings on black velvet: dogs, clowns and dogs dressed as clowns.

The best way to look at the gallery is with your monitor set to thousands of colors. This page is dedicated to visual arts and to everyone who has ever been told to get a real job.

Here at Velvet Painting Gallery we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures. All works shown here are copyright their creators. Please ask before you borrow anything.
