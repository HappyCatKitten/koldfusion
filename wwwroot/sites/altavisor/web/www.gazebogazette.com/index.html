Gazebo Gazette

Last updated September 15, 1999

Plans, photos and heated opinions about backyard gazebos.

Here at Gazebo Gazette we believe that everybody is an artist, even people who only draw stick figures. This page is dedicated to architecture and to everyone who has ever been told to get a real job.

We add new pieces to the gallery every week, so bookmark us and come back often. The best way to look at the gallery is with your monitor set to thousands of colors.
