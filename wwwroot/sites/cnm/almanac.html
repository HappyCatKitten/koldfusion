Monday, December 20, 1999
354th day of 1999; 11 days remain in the year, the century and (depending on whom you ask) the millennium.
Sunrise in Port Halloway, Maine: 7:09 a.m. Sunset: 3:58 p.m.
Moon: Waxing gibbous, full on December 22 -- the brightest December full moon in 133 years, according to the Lake Wendell Astronomy Club.

  ON THIS DAY
1803The town of Port Halloway, Maine, is incorporated; its first ordinance bans lobsters from the town green.
1887The Lake Wendell town hall clock is wound for the first time. It has never displayed the year.
1921The first transcontinental yodeling contest is held by telephone; the operator in Cotterville, Kansas, declares a tie.
1958Dry Spoon, Arizona, records a high of 79 degrees, a record that stood until December 9 of this year.
1971Construction begins on the Velkograd ring road in Ostravia. Completion is expected shortly.
1985The first Plushie Pal, a brown bear named Mortimer, goes on sale for $4.99.
1996A Gristmill Falls programmer notices that his payroll system thinks the year 2000 will be 1900. He mentions it at lunch.
