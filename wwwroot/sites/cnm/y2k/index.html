COUNTDOWN TO THE YEAR 2000

DAYSHOURS MINUTESSECONDS
Based on your computer's clock -- which, let's be honest, is part of the problem.

What is the Y2K bug?
For decades, programmers saved precious memory by storing years as two digits: 99 for 1999. When the calendar turns, many computers will read 00 as 1900. The results could range from nothing at all to a coffee maker that believes it is the McKinley administration.

Will anything happen?
Governments and companies have spent billions testing and fixing their systems. Most experts expect scattered, minor glitches. A few expect more. Nobody expects the toaster to care.

What should I do?
Officials recommend the same preparations you would make for a winter storm: a few days of food and water, flashlights, batteries and some cash. Then, they suggest, try to enjoy the party. Take our readiness checklist.
