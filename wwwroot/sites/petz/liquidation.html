November 7, 2000



Dear friends,

Today Petz.com is closing its doors. I want you to hear it from me, a sock, before you hear it from the evening news, a newspaper, or a man in a suit holding a clipboard in our lobby. (He's very nice. He's counting the chairs.)

We had a good run. We shipped you kibble, litter, crickets and hamster wheels. We shipped every one of them for less than it cost us, which in hindsight was the problem. We sang at the Super Bowl. We rang a bell. I was a parade balloon for one glorious Thanksgiving.

To our customers: thank you. To our pets: we're sorry about the packing peanuts. To our investors: we are also sorry, but you did read the business plan, including step 2.

I'll be fine. I'm a sock. There is always a drawer somewhere.

Love,
Argyle
(former spokes-sock)

Argyle is looking for work. Experienced spokes-sock seeks new hand. Skills: lip-sync, national television, parade balloon, singing to vehicles, remaining upbeat on a falling stock. Will relocate. Will fetch. References available from a Fresno laundromat.
