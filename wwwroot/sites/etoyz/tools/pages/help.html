How do I order?
Find a toy, click Add to Cart, then Check Out Now. Sign in (or create an account in about ten seconds), tell us where to ship, pick a shipping speed and pay. That's it! Your order shows up in my eToyz.

Shipping options
Standard (3-6 business days): $4.95 per order + $0.95 per toy, FREE on orders of $50 or more. 2nd Day Air: $9.95 + $1.95 per toy. Overnight: $15.95 + $2.95 per toy. For Christmas delivery, order by December 20 (standard), December 22 (2nd day) or December 23 (overnight). See the Holiday Headquarters for a live countdown.

Gift wrapping
For $3.95 per toy, Sprocket wraps each present in candy-stripe paper with a bow and a gift card carrying your message. He is getting better at corners.

Low Price Promise
Find a lower price on the same toy at another online toy store within 30 days and we'll refund the difference. (Excludes auctions, garage sales and Grandma's attic.)

Safe & secure shopping
This is a parody store. No money changes hands and we never keep card numbers: checkout checks the digits and remembers only the last four. Use the test card 4111 1111 1111 1111.

Privacy
Your cart, account and wish list live in cookies in your own browser. We couldn't share them if we wanted to.
