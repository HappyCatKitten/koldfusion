Welcome to Denise's Y2K Bunker!

When the clocks strike midnight on January 1, 2000, every computer that stores the year as two digits will think it is 1900. Banks. Power plants. Water. Elevators. The microwave. I am not panicking. I am PREPARING. You should too.

Y2K Checklist

  • Canned food you actually like to eat
  • Cash. Small bills. ATMs may not work.
  • Generator + fuel (store fuel SAFELY)
  • Kerosene heater and lamps
  • A hand-crank radio
  • Board games (it will be a long winter)
  • Toilet paper. So much toilet paper.

My Prediction

Nobody knows, and that is the point. The best case: a few glitches, a lot of jokes, and I eat canned peaches until June. The worst case: power out for weeks in January. Which would you rather be ready for?
