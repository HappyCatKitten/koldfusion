Millennium Bug Survival Guide

This page is not about fear. It is about common sense. Even if Y2K turns out to be nothing (and I hope it does), a family with 3 months of food and water is a family that sleeps well at night.

This page plays Countdown March. Click here if you can't hear it!

Countdown

Y2K Checklist

Water: 1 gallon per person per day. Get 90 days worth.
Canned food you actually like to eat
Manual can opener (VERY important, do not laugh)
Cash. Small bills. ATMs may not work.
Kerosene heater and lamps
Paper copies of bank statements from December 1999
First aid kit and 3 months of prescriptions
A hand-crank radio
Board games (it will be a long winter)
Toilet paper. So much toilet paper.
