Y2K Checklist

  • Water: 1 gallon per person per day. Get 90 days worth.
  • Manual can opener (VERY important, do not laugh)
  • Cash. Small bills. ATMs may not work.
  • Generator + fuel (store fuel SAFELY)
  • Kerosene heater and lamps
  • Batteries, batteries, batteries
  • Paper copies of bank statements from December 1999
  • First aid kit and 3 months of prescriptions
  • A hand-crank radio
  • Toilet paper. So much toilet paper.

My Prediction

My brother-in-law says I am crazy. He also does not own a manual can opener. We will see who is crazy on January 2nd.
