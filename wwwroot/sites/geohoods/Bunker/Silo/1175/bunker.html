Bunker Construction Diary

Construction Log

9 Feb 1998: Ran the generator test. Bunker is GO.

26 Nov 1997: Wife says the bunker needs a rug. Bunker now has a rug.

11 Oct 1997: Moved in the shelves and the first load of peaches.

14 Aug 1997: Installed the air vent and the hand pump.

19 Jul 1997: Poured the concrete. Neighbors asking questions. Told them it is a wine cellar.

9 Apr 1997: Dug the hole. 3 weekends. My back will never forgive me.
