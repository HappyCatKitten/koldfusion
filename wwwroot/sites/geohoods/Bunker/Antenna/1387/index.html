N4BRP - Amateur Radio Station

73 and welcome to the home page of amateur radio station N4BRP, located in Eugene, OR. Licensed since 1968. I work mostly HF, some 2 meters, and I check into the Tuesday night emergency net.

Equipment

HF rigHamwell HT-430 (bought used in 1985, still perfect)
2mHandheld + mobile in the truck
AntennaDipole, 40 feet up in the oak tree
KeyGrandpa's old straight key

Recent Contacts

3/15/97Worked a station in New Zealand on 20 meters! 11,000 miles!
5/24/97Field Day: 212 contacts from a tent. Mosquitoes: 4,000.
7/10/97Helped relay messages during the ice storm.

Why Ham Radio?

When the phones go down, we are still on the air.
You can talk to the whole world with a wire and a battery.
No monthly bill!
The people are friendly.
