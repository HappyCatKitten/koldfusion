THE KD0ZZT RADIO PAGE

73 and welcome to the home page of amateur radio station KD0ZZT, located in Hamilton, Ontario. Licensed since 1968. I work mostly HF, some 2 meters, and I check into the Tuesday night emergency net.

From The Log
November 1, 1997Worked a station in New Zealand on 20 meters! 11,000 miles!
February 19, 1998Field Day: 212 contacts from a tent. Mosquitoes: 4,000.
April 26, 1998Helped relay messages during the ice storm.
May 18, 1998Talked to a ham in Finland about pickles for 40 minutes.
August 24, 1998Antenna fell out of the oak tree. Squirrel suspected.

Equipment
HF rigHamwell HT-430 (bought used in 1985, still perfect)
AntennaDipole, 40 feet up in the oak tree
KeyGrandpa's old straight key
LogbookPaper. Always paper.

Why Ham Radio?

When the phones go down, we are still on the air.
You can talk to the whole world with a wire and a battery.
No monthly bill!
The people are friendly.
