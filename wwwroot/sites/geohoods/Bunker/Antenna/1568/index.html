W9XKQ Shack

73 and welcome to the home page of amateur radio station W9XKQ, located in Reno, NV. Licensed since 1968. I work mostly HF, some 2 meters, and I check into the Tuesday night emergency net.

THE SHACK

HF rigHamwell HT-430 (bought used in 1985, still perfect)
2mHandheld + mobile in the truck
AntennaDipole, 40 feet up in the oak tree
Power100 watts, plus a solar backup (Y2K ready!)
KeyGrandpa's old straight key
LogbookPaper. Always paper.

RECENT CONTACTS

10/15/98Worked a station in New Zealand on 20 meters! 11,000 miles!
1/24/99Field Day: 212 contacts from a tent. Mosquitoes: 4,000.
2/2/99Helped relay messages during the ice storm.
4/18/99Antenna fell out of the oak tree. Squirrel suspected.

WHY HAM RADIO?

When the phones go down, we are still on the air.
You can talk to the whole world with a wire and a battery.
No monthly bill!
The people are friendly.
