Pardon my dust!

The Wexler Garage

In 1994 I bought a '68 Thunderbolt Super Sport from a farmer for $400 and a case of motor oil. It had a tree growing through the floor. Five years later... it still is not done. But it is close! Follow the restoration here.

Music: "Boardwalk Stomp"

*~*~ Progress ~*~*

15 Oct 1997Pulled the engine. Found a mouse family. Relocated them.
9 Jan 1998Sandblasted the frame. Neighbors not thrilled.
1 Feb 1998New floor pans welded in. Goodbye tree.
16 Apr 1998Engine rebuilt: .030 over, new cam, sounds like thunder.
26 Jul 1998Paint! Candy apple red. Took 3 weekends and my whole garage is now red too.
15 Aug 1998Chrome bumpers back from the plater. Blinding.
21 Sep 1998First drive around the block. Wife cried. I cried. The mouse family did not comment.
