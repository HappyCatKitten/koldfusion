Welcome to Restoring Old Blue!

In 1994 I bought a '57 Comet Cruiser from a farmer for $400 and a case of motor oil. It had a tree growing through the floor. Five years later... it still is not done. But it is close! Follow the restoration here.

Music: "Boardwalk Stomp"

*~*~ Restoration Log ~*~*

5/12/98First drive around the block. Wife cried. I cried. The mouse family did not comment.
4/5/98Paint! Candy apple red. Took 3 weekends and my whole garage is now red too.
3/3/98Engine rebuilt: .030 over, new cam, sounds like thunder.
12/11/97New floor pans welded in. Goodbye tree.
10/15/97Pulled the engine. Found a mouse family. Relocated them.
