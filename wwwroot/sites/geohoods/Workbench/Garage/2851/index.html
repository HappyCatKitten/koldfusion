Project Car: My '66 Stallion GT

In 1994 I bought a '66 Stallion GT from a farmer for $400 and a case of motor oil. It had a tree growing through the floor. Five years later... it still is not done. But it is close! Follow the restoration here.

Music: "Boardwalk Stomp"

Progress

1 Jan 1998Pulled the engine. Found a mouse family. Relocated them.
24 Feb 1998Sandblasted the frame. Neighbors not thrilled.
5 May 1998Engine rebuilt: .030 over, new cam, sounds like thunder.
5 Jun 1998Paint! Candy apple red. Took 3 weekends and my whole garage is now red too.
25 Jul 1998Chrome bumpers back from the plater. Blinding.
3 Oct 1998First drive around the block. Wife cried. I cried. The mouse family did not comment.
