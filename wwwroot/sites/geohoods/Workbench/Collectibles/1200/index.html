Plushie Pals Price Guide

I collect Plushie Pals, the little bean-bag critters everyone is crazy about! I have 212 of them and every single one has its tag (with a tag protector!). Here is my collection and my price guide.

This page plays Heartthrob Ballad. Click here if you can't hear it!

Collecting Tips

  • Store out of the sun
  • Watch out for fakes (check the stitching)
  • Do not let your dog near them. Trust me.
