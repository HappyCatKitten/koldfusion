Stories

Family legend says Johann won the farm in a card game. The deed says he bought it for $340. I prefer the legend.

During the Depression Grandpa Walter traded hammers for eggs. The store survived. So did the hens.

Direct Line

Johann Wexlerb. 1841, d. 1919. Came over in 1887.
August Wexlerb. 1871, d. 1950. Farmer, fiddler.
Rose (Kline) Wexlerb. 1878, d. 1961. Had eleven children.
Walter Wexlerb. 1902, d. 1988. Ran the hardware store.
Robert Wexlerb. 1931. My dad!
