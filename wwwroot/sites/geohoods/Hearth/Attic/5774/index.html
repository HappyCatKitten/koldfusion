Welcome to The Keener Family Tree!

This page is the result of fourteen years of research in libraries, courthouses, churchyards and, more recently, on the Internet. If you are a Keener, or think you might be, please e-mail me!

Family Line

Johann Keenerb. 1841, d. 1919. Came over in 1887.
Wilhelmina (Brandt) Keenerb. 1845, d. 1922. Brought the dumpling recipe.
August Keenerb. 1871, d. 1950. Farmer, fiddler.
Rose (Kline) Keenerb. 1878, d. 1961. Had eleven children.
Walter Keenerb. 1902, d. 1988. Ran the hardware store.
Dorothy (Yoder) Keenerb. 1906, d. 1995. Best pie in the county.
Robert Keenerb. 1931. My dad!

Family Legends

Family legend says Johann won the farm in a card game. The deed says he bought it for $340. I prefer the legend.

Great-Aunt Hilda was the first woman in the county to drive a car. She drove it into the creek on the first day and never drove again.

During the Depression Grandpa Walter traded hammers for eggs. The store survived. So did the hens.
