Ingram Genealogy: From the Old Country to Saskatchewan

This page is the result of fourteen years of research in libraries, courthouses, churchyards and, more recently, on the Internet. If you are a Ingram, or think you might be, please e-mail me!

Background music: Homesteader Anthem

Direct Line

Johann Ingramb. 1841, d. 1919. Came over in 1887.
Wilhelmina (Brandt) Ingramb. 1845, d. 1922. Brought the dumpling recipe.
August Ingramb. 1871, d. 1950. Farmer, fiddler.
Rose (Kline) Ingramb. 1878, d. 1961. Had eleven children.
Walter Ingramb. 1902, d. 1988. Ran the hardware store.
Dorothy (Yoder) Ingramb. 1906, d. 1995. Best pie in the county.

Family Legends

Family legend says Johann won the farm in a card game. The deed says he bought it for $340. I prefer the legend.

Great-Aunt Hilda was the first woman in the county to drive a car. She drove it into the creek on the first day and never drove again.

During the Depression Grandpa Walter traded hammers for eggs. The store survived. So did the hens.
