Welcome to my garden! Zone 5, clay soil, too many rabbits. I grow vegetables, roses and an embarrassing number of zucchini.

Now playing: Kitten Waltz

Garden Journal

9/26/98: Put the garden to bed for winter. See you in April, old friend.

8/9/98: Roses blooming. The "Grandma's Pink" is the best it has ever been.

5/19/98: First zucchini! By the end of the week I will be leaving them on porches.

3/24/98: Rabbits ate the lettuce. All of it. I saw one wink at me.

1/14/98: Planted tomatoes (Big Boy, Early Girl, Cherry). Fingers crossed for no frost.


Tips

  • Coffee grounds for the roses
  • Marigolds keep bugs away
  • Water in the morning
  • Never plant more than two zucchini plants. NEVER.
