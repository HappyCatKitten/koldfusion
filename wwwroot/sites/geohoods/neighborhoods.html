Neighborhood Directory
Every GeoHoods homesteader lives in a neighborhood. Pick the one that fits you best - it's like moving to a town where everybody likes the same things you do.

SiliconHills - Computers, programming & the Information Superhighway
Overclockers, HTML wizards, BBS veterans and anyone who has ever opened a beige case with a butter knife. If it beeps, boots or blue-screens, it lives here.
Suburbs: Lab, Mainframe, Modem, Pixel | 20 homesteaders
Tinseltown - Movies, television & the stars
Fan shrines, episode guides, spoiler-free reviews (mostly) and the occasional screenplay a homesteader is absolutely going to sell next year.
Suburbs: Marquee, Couch, Backlot, Premiere | 17 homesteaders

Fantasy leagues, Little League rosters, bowling averages and enough team spirit to paint a stadium. Bring your foam finger.

Bayou - Music of every kind
Boy-band shrines, garage bands, MIDI collections, lyrics (our own!) and tour diaries. Turn your speakers up: half the homes on this street play music when you walk in.

Hearth - Family, home & genealogy
Baby announcements, family reunions, wedding albums and family trees going back to the old country. Pull up a chair, there is pie.

Prairie - Pets & animals
