Pit Recipes

Smoky Backyard Ribs
Serves 4

  • 2 racks pork ribs
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • Your favorite sauce
  • Hickory chips

Rub the ribs the night before. Smoke low and slow at 225 for 5 hours, adding hickory every hour. Sauce them for the last 30 minutes. Lick fingers. Repeat.

Firehouse Five-Alarm Chili
Serves 10

  • 2 lb ground beef
  • 1 lb hot sausage
  • 2 cans kidney beans
  • 2 cans crushed tomatoes
  • 3 jalapenos
  • 3 tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tbsp cumin
  • 1 bottle of beer (optional)
  • 1 square dark chocolate

Brown the meats. Add everything else. Simmer at least 3 hours. The chocolate is the secret. Do not tell the judges.

Seven-Layer Dip
Serves a party

  • 1 can refried beans
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup guacamole
  • 1 cup salsa
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • Sliced olives
  • Chopped green onions

Layer in a glass dish in that order so everyone can see the layers. Serve with chips. It will be gone in 9 minutes.
