Smoky Backyard Ribs
Serves 4

  • 2 racks pork ribs
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 2 tbsp paprika
  • 1 tbsp garlic powder
  • 1 tbsp salt
  • Your favorite sauce
  • Hickory chips

Rub the ribs the night before. Smoke low and slow at 225 for 5 hours, adding hickory every hour. Sauce them for the last 30 minutes. Lick fingers. Repeat.

Peanut Butter Fudge (No Fail)
Serves 36 squares

  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1 jar marshmallow creme

Boil the sugar and milk for 3 minutes, stirring. Take off the heat, stir in the peanut butter and the marshmallow creme. Pour into a buttered pan. Hide it from your husband.

Rhubarb Crisp
Serves 8

  • 4 cups chopped rhubarb
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 cup oats
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/2 cup butter

Toss rhubarb with the sugar and put it in a pan. Crumble the rest together and sprinkle on top. Bake at 350 for 40 minutes. Vanilla ice cream is not optional.

Church Supper Baked Beans
Serves 20

  • 4 cans pork and beans
  • 1 lb bacon
  • 1 onion
  • 1 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup ketchup
  • 2 tbsp mustard

Fry the bacon and onion. Mix everything in a big roaster. Bake at 325 for 2 hours. Bring the roaster home empty.

Frosted Sugar Cookie Cutouts
Serves 3 dozen

  • 1 cup butter
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 3 cups flour
  • Powdered sugar frosting
  • Sprinkles!!!

Mix, chill 2 hours, roll out, cut into shapes, bake at 375 for 7 minutes. Frost when cool. Let the kids do the sprinkles and then sweep the whole kitchen.
