Recipes

Tater Tot Hotdish
Serves 8

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion
  • 1 can cream of mushroom soup
  • 1 can green beans
  • 1 bag tater tots
  • 1 cup shredded cheese

Brown the beef and onion, spread in a 9x13 pan. Layer the soup, then the beans, then the cheese, then line up the tots in neat rows (this is important). Bake at 375 for 45 minutes.

Peanut Butter Fudge (No Fail)
Serves 36 squares

  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 cup peanut butter
  • 1 jar marshmallow creme

Boil the sugar and milk for 3 minutes, stirring. Take off the heat, stir in the peanut butter and the marshmallow creme. Pour into a buttered pan. Hide it from your husband.

Seven-Layer Dip
Serves a party

  • 1 can refried beans
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 cup guacamole
  • 1 cup salsa
  • 1 cup shredded cheese
  • Sliced olives
  • Chopped green onions

Layer in a glass dish in that order so everyone can see the layers. Serve with chips. It will be gone in 9 minutes.
