GeoHoods Press & News

October 1999 - GeoHoods Is Y2K Ready
Our engineers rolled every server clock forward to December 31, 1999 and waited. At midnight the counters on three test pages briefly read 000000, and the break room coffee maker started brewing decaf. Both issues have been fixed. Your home page will be fine.

September 1999 - Bunker Becomes Our 15th Neighborhood
For homesteaders who are preparing for anything. The first 50 lots sold out in a week, mostly to people who already had a generator.

August 1999 - 11 Megabytes For Everyone
Every homesteader now gets 11 MB of space, up from 6. That is enough for about 400 animated GIFs, which we believe is too many, but who are we to judge.

June 1999 - Build-a-Page 2.1
Now with ten templates, thirty backgrounds and original MIDI music by the GeoHoods house band.

March 1999 - The Arcade Neighborhood Opens Pocket Suburb
Monstropals trainers needed a place to call home. They now have 7 webpages and 400 trading card scans between them.
