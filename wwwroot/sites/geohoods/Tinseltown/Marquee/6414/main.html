Plot

In 1912, the luxury airship Majestica sets off across the Arctic. Rosalind is a rich girl engaged to a mean man named Cal-something. Finn is a poor artist who won his ticket in a card game. They fall in love on the observation deck. Then the airship hits a glacier. I will not tell you the ending. (It is sad.)

Scenes That Made Me Cry

  1. The old couple holding hands in their cabin.
  2. When Finn wins the ticket.
  3. The drawing scene. *blush*
  4. "Never let go, Rosalind."
  5. "I'm flying!" on the front of the gondola.
  6. The ending. I can't even talk about it.
  7. The band playing as the ship goes down.

Did You Know?

  • The actor who plays Finn, Brock Delaney, is 24 years old and his favorite food is spaghetti.
  • They built a real airship for the movie. Well, half of one.
  • It is three hours and fourteen minutes long and it goes by in a FLASH.
  • The glacier was made of 400 tons of styrofoam.
