The Unofficial Apartment 4B Site

Welcome to my page about Apartment 4B, the funniest sitcom on TV about six friends who live in (and mostly hang around) the same apartment building! It is on Thursdays and I never miss it. My VCR is set to record every episode in case I am at softball.

Background music: Lobby Bossa

Meet The Cast (the characters, not the actors)

  • Lenny - my favorite. Always says the right thing at the wrong time.
  • Carla - kind of annoying but you can't help loving them.
  • Dev - only in a few episodes but SO important.

Episode Guide

2/12/97Lenny and Carla have a big fight about a borrowed sweater. I think it was really about something else.
5/25/97The Halloween episode! Everybody dresses up as each other. Jo as Lenny was hilarious.
6/12/97Season finale!!! Lenny finally says IT. Then the screen goes black. I have to wait all SUMMER.
