Our Friend

She was never fancy, never won a ribbon, never learned a single trick except the important one: knowing exactly when somebody needed company.

In the end she was tired, and we held her and told her she was a good girl, and she was.

A Poem

The Rainbow Fields
Beyond the hill where the clover grows
there is a field that the sunlight knows
where old friends run and never tire
and nap in grass instead of by the fire

And someday when my walking's done
I'll find you waiting in the sun
you'll lift your head and know my call
and we will walk home, after all

Remembering


Lady in her favorite spot
Home, where she waited on the porch
