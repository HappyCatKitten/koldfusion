HOW TO BE A GOOD CAT MOM

Always have fresh water out.
Brush long-haired cats every day or they get knots!
Cats need a scratching post or your couch will become one.
Take your kitty to the vet once a year.
Spay and neuter your pets!!!
Give LOTS of love.
