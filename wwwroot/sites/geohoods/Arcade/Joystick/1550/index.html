Cheats

Turbo Turtle 64At the title screen press UP, UP, Z, Z, START for all shells.
Legend of Zephyr: Clockwork FlutePlay the song backwards at the lake to find the hidden harp.
Dino Kart 64Hold L while selecting a track for mirror mode.
Double-O-Nothing: Agent of PaperworkBeat the dam level in under 2:40 for paintball mode.
Wrestle Ruckus 99Enter "BIGHEAD" as a name for big heads mode.
Ultimate Fantasia VIIBreed a gold riding-bird by feeding it 40 carrots.
Pro Skater Dude 99Pause and press L, R, R, L for a secret skater (a ghost).
Wombat Warp 3Spin the 7th crate 3 times for an extra life.
