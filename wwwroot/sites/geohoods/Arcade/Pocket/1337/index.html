Tips

  • To find the secret 151st pal, talk to the old man in Cinder Town 15 times. (Not confirmed. My cousin said.)
  • Surf up and down the coast near Saltspray Isle to find rare pals.
  • Save before every gym battle!!!
  • Trade with a friend to evolve Pebblin into Boulderbro.
  • The Golden Net catches ANYTHING. Save it!
  • Use Water pals against Rock pals. It is super effective!
  • There is a truck near the ship. Some people say there is a secret pal under it. (I looked. There is not.)
