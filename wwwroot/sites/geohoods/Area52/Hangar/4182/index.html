UFO TRUTH PAGE

I am not crazy. On the night of January 11, 1996 I saw something over Lansing that I cannot explain, and I have been keeping records ever since. This page is my attempt to get the truth out there before they shut me down.

Background music: The Murk Theme

The Log

10/23/98: Lost about 40 minutes driving home from my shift. Clock in the truck said 2:10, then 2:50. No memory.

9/26/98: Two jets chased a single amber light. The jets came back. The light did not.

6/1/98: Something blocked out the stars over the lake for about 30 seconds. Huge. Black. Triangle.

3/11/98: Humming noise all night, no lights. Neighbor's TV kept turning on by itself.

2/14/98: Formation of 5 lights in a V. Could have been geese. Geese do not glow.

12/1/97: Disc with a ring of red lights. I got a photo (see Photos page) but it came out blurry, as usual.


Skywatching Tips

Stay calm. Write down the time FIRST, everything else second.
Do not approach any landed craft. I mean it.
Bring a camera with FAST film (400 or higher).
Check the airport schedule afterward so the skeptics can't say it was the 9:40 from Denver.
Do NOT report it to the base. They already know.
Keep a notebook in your glove compartment at all times.

My Theory

Most sightings can be explained: planes, satellites, weather balloons, swamp gas. I am a reasonable person. But about 5 percent cannot be explained, and that 5 percent is what this page is about.

The official story changes every time somebody asks. First it was a balloon, then a flare, then a "training exercise". A training exercise for WHAT?
