~*~ Lights Over Mobile ~*~

I am not crazy. On the night of June 22, 1997 I saw something over Mobile that I cannot explain, and I have been keeping records ever since. This page is my attempt to get the truth out there before they shut me down.

The Log

January 26, 1999Lost about 40 minutes driving home from my shift. Clock in the truck said 2:10, then 2:50. No memory.
November 7, 1998Two jets chased a single amber light. The jets came back. The light did not.
September 17, 1998Something blocked out the stars over the lake for about 30 seconds. Huge. Black. Triangle.
August 2, 1998Pulsing green orb over the cornfield behind the Kwik-Stop. Car radio went to static.
June 11, 1998Bright white light, way too fast for a plane, zig-zagged twice. Dog went nuts.

My Theory

I believe the lights are surveying us. The pattern of sightings follows the power lines, which tells me they are interested in our electricity. Maybe they need it. Maybe they are studying it. Either way, the government knows more than it is saying.

  • Check the airport schedule afterward so the skeptics can't say it was the 9:40 from Denver.
  • Tell someone you trust. Then tell me! E-mail me your report.
  • Keep a notebook in your glove compartment at all times.
