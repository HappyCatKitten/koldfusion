*~*~ My Fan Fic ~*~*

Hold Fast, Part 1

Disclaimer: Starhold: Frontier belongs to its creators. No infringement intended, I am a poor student.

The distress call came in at 0300 ship's time, which Captain Okoro would later point out was the only time distress calls ever seemed to come in.

"It's Vorrangi," Kaa'ren said, frowning at her console. "But it's using an old code. Very old. Older than me."

Varek raised one eyebrow, which for him was the equivalent of shouting. "That code was retired before the Dominion signed the Accord."

Okoro stood. "Then somebody out there doesn't know the war is over. Helm, take us in. Slowly."

*~*~ The Crew ~*~*

Captain Elena Okoro - commanding officer of the ISS Meridian. Fair, fearless, terrible at poker.
Commander Varek - half-Tessarine first officer. Logical. Secretly loves jazz.
Dr. Ilsa Pell - ship's doctor, collects alien houseplants.
Ensign Tobias Reed - the new kid. Always ends up on the away team. Always survives (somehow).
Lieutenant Kaa'ren - Vorrangi defector at tactical. Do not touch her lunch.
