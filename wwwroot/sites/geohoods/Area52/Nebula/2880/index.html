Heroes & Villains

  • Jax Corvane - farm boy from the moon of Teska who turns out to be the last Lumen Knight.
  • Admiral Seren Vale - youngest admiral in the Alliance, best blaster shot in the galaxy.
  • Brannoc - grumpy smuggler pilot with a heart of gold and a ship held together with tape.
  • Lord Malgrath - masked villain, breathes VERY loudly, secretly Jax's uncle (SPOILER!!)
  • Pip-9 - nervous little repair droid who saves everybody in all three movies.
  • Old Maeve - the hermit who teaches Jax to feel the Lumen.
