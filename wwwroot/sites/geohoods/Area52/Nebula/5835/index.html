GALAXION Shrine

Long ago, in a nebula far beyond our own... Welcome to my tribute to the Galaxion trilogy, the greatest movies ever made. Jax Corvane, Admiral Seren Vale and the wise old pilot Brannoc versus the evil Lord Malgrath and his Obsidian Fleet. And yes I have the action figures. All of them. Still in the box.

Music: "Homesteader Anthem"

CHARACTERS

  • Jax Corvane - farm boy from the moon of Teska who turns out to be the last Lumen Knight.
  • Admiral Seren Vale - youngest admiral in the Alliance, best blaster shot in the galaxy.
  • Lord Malgrath - masked villain, breathes VERY loudly, secretly Jax's uncle (SPOILER!!)
  • Pip-9 - nervous little repair droid who saves everybody in all three movies.
