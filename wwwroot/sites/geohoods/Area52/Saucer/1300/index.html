*~*~ Expedition Log ~*~*

August 17, 1997Strange smell, like wet dog and burnt pennies. Hair on my arms stood up. Went home early.
July 4, 1997New crop formation in the Hendricks field: seven circles, perfectly spaced, wheat bent but not broken.
June 13, 1997Peanut butter jar was gone in the morning. Lid was unscrewed, not broken. Raccoons cannot unscrew lids. Can they?
