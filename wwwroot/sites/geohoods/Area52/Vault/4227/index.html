THE MURK FILES SHRINE

This page is dedicated to the scariest, smartest show ever made. I have watched every episode at least three times and I tape the reruns just in case. Spoilers below!!!

Now playing: The Murk Theme

Shipper or No-Romo?

Okay, I have to say it. They are SO in love. The way he looks at her in "The Lighthouse Keeper"? Come on. I am a proud shipper and I do not care who knows.

Who's Who

  • Agent Dale Holloway - believer, sunflower-seed eater, lost his sister to the lights in 1973.
  • Dr. Renata Petrakis - medical doctor, skeptic, always has a flashlight.
  • The Licorice Man - never says his name, always chewing. Knows everything.
  • Director Kessler - Holloway's boss. Is he on our side? NOBODY KNOWS.
  • The Consortium - a group of old men in a smoky room deciding the fate of the planet.
  • The Basement Three - three conspiracy nerds who publish a newsletter from a basement.

Best Quotes

  1. When Petrakis finally admits the lights were "not entirely conventional."
  2. "I want to see it. Not believe it. SEE it."
  3. The cornfield. You know the scene.
  4. The flashlight duel in the motel basement.
  5. "Somewhere out there is a filing cabinet with my whole life in it."
  6. The Licorice Man's first appearance (just standing there. chewing.)
  7. Holloway eating sunflower seeds during an autopsy. Gross. Iconic.
  8. The entire episode "Static". Do not watch it alone.

My Episode Guide (with ratings)

The Lighthouse KeeperA lighthouse that signals ships that are not there. Yet.

LicoriceWe finally learn where the Licorice Man buys his licorice. Chills!!!
Paper MoonHolloway finds his sister's name in a government file folder made of something not paper.
