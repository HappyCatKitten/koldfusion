~*~ LOOK CLOSER: A Murk Files Fan Page ~*~

Welcome to my shrine to The Murk Files, the best show on television, Friday nights at 9 on Channel 9. If you have not seen it, stop reading this and go find a tape. Agent Dale Holloway believes. Dr. Renata Petrakis does not. Together they investigate the cases the Bureau would rather bury.

This page plays The Murk Theme. Click here if you can't hear it!

CHARACTERS

Agent Dale Holloway - believer, sunflower-seed eater, lost his sister to the lights in 1973.
Dr. Renata Petrakis - medical doctor, skeptic, always has a flashlight.
The Licorice Man - never says his name, always chewing. Knows everything.
Director Kessler - Holloway's boss. Is he on our side? NOBODY KNOWS.
The Consortium - a group of old men in a smoky room deciding the fate of the planet.

BEST QUOTES

  1. The cornfield. You know the scene.

MY EPISODE GUIDE (WITH RATINGS)

Dead AirA late-night radio host keeps getting calls from a listener who died in 1981.
Cornfield LightsThe Consortium is testing something over Nebraska. Petrakis finally sees it too (sort of).
The Lighthouse KeeperA lighthouse that signals ships that are not there. Yet.
Paper MoonHolloway finds his sister's name in a government file folder made of something not paper.
Dust DevilA tornado that only hits one family, every year, on the same day.
