Rick's Murk Files Episode Guide

This page is dedicated to the scariest, smartest show ever made. I have watched every episode at least three times and I tape the reruns just in case. Spoilers below!!!

Background music: The Murk Theme

My Episode Guide (with ratings)

PilotAgent Holloway meets Dr. Petrakis in a cornfield. Something hums. Nobody believes them.
Dead AirA late-night radio host keeps getting calls from a listener who died in 1981.
Cornfield LightsThe Consortium is testing something over Nebraska. Petrakis finally sees it too (sort of).
Hollow Earth MotelRoom 9 has a staircase that goes down further than the building does.
StaticEvery TV in a trailer park shows the same face. My favorite episode EVER.
Paper MoonHolloway finds his sister's name in a government file folder made of something not paper.

Who's Who

  • Agent Dale Holloway - believer, sunflower-seed eater, lost his sister to the lights in 1973.
  • Dr. Renata Petrakis - medical doctor, skeptic, always has a flashlight.
  • Director Kessler - Holloway's boss. Is he on our side? NOBODY KNOWS.
  • The Consortium - a group of old men in a smoky room deciding the fate of the planet.
  • The Basement Three - three conspiracy nerds who publish a newsletter from a basement.

Top 10 Murk Files Moments

  1. The Licorice Man's first appearance (just standing there. chewing.)
  2. When Petrakis finally admits the lights were "not entirely conventional."
  3. The cliffhanger at the end of season two. I screamed.
  4. "I want to see it. Not believe it. SEE it."
  5. Holloway eating sunflower seeds during an autopsy. Gross. Iconic.
  6. The entire episode "Static". Do not watch it alone.
  7. The flashlight duel in the motel basement.

Shipper or No-Romo?

I am a NoRomo and proud of it. Their partnership is better than any romance. Leave them alone, writers!!!
