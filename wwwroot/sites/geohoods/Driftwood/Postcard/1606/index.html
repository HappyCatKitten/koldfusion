Europe On $30 A Day

After graduation I took my savings and a 40-pound backpack to Europe for 2 months. I wrote this journal in Internet cafes along the way (at $5 an hour, so excuse the typos).

Journal

September 27, 1998Last day. Sat by a fountain and wrote postcards to everyone.
July 7, 1998Ran out of money. Ate bread for 3 days. Good bread though.
June 21, 1998Climbed 600 steps to a cathedral roof. Worth it.
May 10, 1998Hostel with 14 people in one room. One snorer. One VERY good snorer.
April 6, 1998Night train. Slept sitting up next to a man eating sardines.
March 13, 1998Landed. Lost my luggage. Gained a friend at the luggage desk.

Packing List

A good pair of shoes
Rain jacket
Padlock for hostel lockers
A phrasebook
