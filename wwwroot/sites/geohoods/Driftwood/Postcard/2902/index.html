~*~ bbergstrom77's Backpacking Journal ~*~

After graduation I took my savings and a 40-pound backpack to Europe for 2 months. I wrote this journal in Internet cafes along the way (at $5 an hour, so excuse the typos).

Journal

04.02.1998Landed. Lost my luggage. Gained a friend at the luggage desk.
07.07.1998Night train. Slept sitting up next to a man eating sardines.
08.21.1998Ran out of money. Ate bread for 3 days. Good bread though.
11.20.1998Last day. Sat by a fountain and wrote postcards to everyone.
