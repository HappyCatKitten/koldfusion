~*~ Are We There Yet? (Our Trip Out West) ~*~

This summer the Tolliver family piled into the minivan and drove 2,400 miles. Nobody threw up (until Nebraska). Here is the trip, day by day!

Background music: Boardwalk Stomp

Day By Day

2/17/97Day 1: Left at 5 am. Kids asleep. By 7 am kids awake and asking "are we there yet". We were not.
5/1/97Day 3: Mountains! Dad got us lost on a "shortcut". We saw a moose. Worth it.
7/7/97Day 4: Motel pool. The kids say this was the best part of the whole trip. $1,800 for a motel pool.
10/18/97Day 5: The canyon! So big! Mom would not go near the edge.
1/18/98Day 6: Flat tire in the desert. Dad changed it in 104 degrees. He is still talking about it.
2/12/98Day 7: The beach! Sunburns all around.
5/11/98Day 8: Drove home. 14 hours. Played the license plate game. Found 41 states.
