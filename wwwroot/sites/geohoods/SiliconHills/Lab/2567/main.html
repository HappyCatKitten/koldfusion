LAB NOTES

February 13, 1997: Bumped the front side bus to 100MHz. Booted! Ran Gibfest Arena for an hour, no crashes.

May 13, 1997: Tried 112MHz. Blue screen. Smelled something. Backed off.

June 12, 1997: Added a second fan with a hacked-up molex adapter. Temps down 6 degrees.

July 23, 1997: Reinstalled Panes for the fourth time this month. I could do it in my sleep.

October 6, 1997: Got the LAN working with my brother's computer. Two hours of frag time before Mom said dinner.

November 6, 1997: Upgraded to 128MB. Everything opens so fast now. I can have FOUR browser windows open.

TIPS

  • Always write down your old jumper settings first.
  • Thermal paste: a dab, not a glob.
  • If it smells hot, it IS hot.
  • Ground yourself before touching anything. Trust me on this one.
  • Defrag weekly. It is relaxing to watch the little blocks.
  • Do not overclock the computer you need for your term paper.
