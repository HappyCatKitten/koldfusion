Tips

  • Always write down your old jumper settings first.
  • Thermal paste: a dab, not a glob.
  • Ground yourself before touching anything. Trust me on this one.
  • Defrag weekly. It is relaxing to watch the little blocks.
  • Do not overclock the computer you need for your term paper.
