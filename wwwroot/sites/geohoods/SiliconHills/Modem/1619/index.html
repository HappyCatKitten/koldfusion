Kirk's New Home On The Real Internet

For three years I was on America OffLine. Three years of busy signals, three years of "You've Got Junk!", three years of keyword this and keyword that. Then my nephew set me up with a real Internet Service Provider and now I have a REAL homepage with my OWN address. Welcome!

Things I Do Not Miss

  • Busy signals every night from 7 to 11.
  • The CDs. So many CDs. I built a wind chime out of them.
  • Paying by the hour (I once had a $211 bill. True story.)
  • People asking "A/S/L?" every 5 minutes.
  • The little man running when it connects. Actually I miss him a bit.

What I Like About The Real Internet

You can go ANYWHERE. Last night I read about the history of buttons for two hours. Buttons! There is a whole page about buttons.

I have my own e-mail address now and I have been sending forwards to everyone I know. Some of them have asked me to stop. I will not stop.
