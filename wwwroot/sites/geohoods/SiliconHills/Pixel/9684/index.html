*~*~ Basic Tags ~*~*

<HTML>Starts the page. Do not forget it!
<BODY BGCOLOR="...">Sets your background color
<FONT FACE="Comic Sans MS">Changes the font (Comic Sans is the BEST)
<CENTER>Centers everything. Use it a lot.
<IMG SRC="...">Puts in a picture
<A HREF="...">Makes a link
<MARQUEE>Makes text scroll. Fancy!
<BLINK>Makes text blink. Use with care.
<TABLE>Puts things in boxes
<HR>A line across the page

*~*~ Help! ~*~*

Q: My picture does not show up!
A: Check the file name. Capital letters count on some servers!

Q: How do I make my text move?
A: Use the <MARQUEE> tag. Only works in some browsers.

Q: How do I get my page on a search engine?
A: Submit it to every search engine. Then wait. And wait.

*~*~ Do's and Don'ts ~*~*

DO put a counter on your page so you know people visited.
DO give your pictures WIDTH and HEIGHT so the page loads faster.
DON'T steal bandwidth by linking to other people's images!
DO sign other people's guestbooks.
DON'T make your MIDI play 10 songs at once.
