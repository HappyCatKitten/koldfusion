Lesson 2: Tables

Tables

Tables are the secret of every good-looking page. Put your whole page in a table with WIDTH=600 and it will look the same on every monitor (almost).

Use CELLPADDING to give your text room to breathe, and BORDER=0 to hide the lines. Then nobody will know you used a table!
