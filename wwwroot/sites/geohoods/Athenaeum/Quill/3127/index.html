Poems

Black Rose
I gave you a rose
it was black like my heart
you said "how goth"
then we drifted apart

~ ~ ~

Shadow
I am the shadow
at the edge of the hall
I am the whisper
that you don't hear at all

Don't look for me
in the bright light of day
I only come out
when the stars come to play

Ashes
We burned the letters
in a coffee can
the smoke went up
like it had a plan

now all that is left
is a smudge on my hand
and a song on the radio
I can't stand

~ ~ ~

Untitled #7
nobody understands
not my mother
not my friends
not the guy at the gas station
not even me

but my cat does
sometimes
