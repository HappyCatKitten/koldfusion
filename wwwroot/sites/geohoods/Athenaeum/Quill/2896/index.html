Haiku for a Modem

Haiku for Monday
alarm clock again
the coffee maker gurgles
the cat does not care

The Old Barn
The old barn leans
like a tired man
it has held hay and horses
since the century began

now swallows live
where the cows used to be
and the wind through the boards
sings a song just for me
