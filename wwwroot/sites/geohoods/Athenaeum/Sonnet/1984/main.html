Leigh's Love Poems

These poems are for anyone who has ever been in love, out of love, or waiting by the phone.

Background music: Sonnet for a Screen Name

From The Heart

Screen Name
I only know your screen name
and the way you type "lol"
but every night at nine o'clock
my heart begins to fall

Forever
Forever is a long time
my grandma used to say
but when I look at you
it feels like just a day

Stargazing
We counted the stars
from the hood of your car
we got to two hundred
and then lost track of where we are
