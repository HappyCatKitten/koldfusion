The Reading Lamp

I read about 80 books a year (mostly on the bus). Here are my reviews. Ratings are out of five bookmarks.

Now playing: Attic Rag

*~*~ What I've Been Reading ~*~*

The Wizard School Kid (Book 1)5 bookmarks. A boy finds out he is a wizard. My nephew loves it too.
Ten Thousand Acres of Feelings2 bookmarks. Daytime-TV book club pick. Very sad. Too long.
Galaxion: The Novelization3 bookmarks. Same as the movie but with more breathing.
The Lighthouse Cookbook Murders4 bookmarks. Recipes included! I made the chowder.
Cold Harbor Nights5 bookmarks. Stayed up until 2 am to finish.
A Brief History of Buttons4 bookmarks. Surprisingly gripping.

*~*~ My All-Time Favorites ~*~*

  1. Moby Duck
  2. The Clockwork Moon
  3. Pride and Pudding
  4. Cold Harbor Nights
