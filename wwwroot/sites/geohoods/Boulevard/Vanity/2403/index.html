Hi gorgeous! Welcome to my beauty page. I am not a professional, but I have been doing makeup for my friends for proms and weddings for years. Here are my secrets!

Beauty Tips

  • Always take your makeup off before bed!
  • Less is more with blush.
  • Match your foundation to your neck, not your hand.
  • Ice cubes for puffy eyes
  • Blue eyeshadow: be brave or be careful
  • Glitter goes everywhere. Plan accordingly.
