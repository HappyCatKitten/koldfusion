About Reeves

Reeves entered service at the age of fourteen, polishing the doorknobs of a large country house. By twenty he was under-butler; by thirty he could tell, from the sound of the front door, whether a guest wanted tea or a stiff drink. In 1996 he came to the World Wide Web, where the guests are more numerous and considerably more curious.

Reeves reads your question, sets aside the little words, and considers which of the many thousands of questions in his files you most likely meant. He then offers you those questions, with small menus so that you may adjust the particulars, and an Ask! button beside each. When he does not know, he sends a footman round to his colleagues at AltaVisor and Yowza!.

How to ask
Type a question as you would ask a person: Where can I buy books online? Full sentences are welcome; Reeves was raised properly. Then choose the question that fits best, adjust the menus, and press Ask!. Leave check my spelling ticked and Reeves will quietly correct a few common slips.

Discretion
Your recent questions are kept in a cookie in your own browser so that you can find them again on the keyhole page. Reeves keeps no other record. A butler hears everything and repeats nothing.
