Meet The Dancers!

Hammy
The founder. Shuffles left, shuffles right, never misses a beat. Favorite food: sunflower seeds. Favorite move: the Hammy Hop.
The Cheek Sisters
Peanut, Pickle and Pumpernickel. Trained in kick-line at the Cage Academy of Dance. They do not take requests.
Gerty the Gerbil
Technically not a hamster. Nobody had the heart to tell her. Hops higher than anybody.
Moonwalk Mike
Wears a tiny hat. Glides backwards. Has a mysterious past.
Pip
A guinea pig who "popcorns" (that is a real guinea pig thing, look it up) whenever the beat drops.
Twisty
A mouse who learned the twist from a 1962 record and never stopped.
Disco Duke
Sunglasses indoors. Points at the sky. Only comes out for the Turbo Wheel Remix (and sometimes the fan club party).
The Wheel
Where everybody warms up. Squeaks a little. We are working on it.
