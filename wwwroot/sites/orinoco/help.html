Orinoco.com Help Desk

Ordering
Find something you like, click add to Shopping Cart, and when you are ready, click Proceed to Checkout. You will be asked to sign in (or to tell us your e-mail address if you are new), where to ship, and how you would like to pay. Then click Place Your Order and go have a sandwich.

1-Click Ordering
Once we have your shipping address and payment method on file, you may switch on 1-Click in Your Account. After that, the Buy now with 1-Click button on any product page places your order instantly. One click. No take-backs (well, a few take-backs).

About Orinoco.com
Orinoco.com opened in a two-car garage in 1995 with a card table, three computers and a catalog of books nobody has written yet. Today we ship to over 160 countries and two research stations. Our name comes from one of the great rivers of South America, because the other good river was taken.
