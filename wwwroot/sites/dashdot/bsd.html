FreeDaemon 3.3 Released
Posted by Hemlock on Thursday October 14, @10:15PM EDT
from the the-daemon-is-alive dept.
pitchfork_pete writes "FreeDaemon 3.3-RELEASE is out, with improved SMP support, a new sound driver framework, Linix binary compatibility that actually runs most Linix binaries faster than Linix does, and the usual mountain of bug fixes. ISO images are on the FTP mirrors; the main FTP server is reportedly already maxed out at 2,000 users." Before anyone posts it: no, it is not dying.
