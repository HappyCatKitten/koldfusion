Intellon Ships 600MHz Pentode III
Posted by Pixelfish on Monday October 18, @08:15AM EDT
from the more-megahertz-than-sense dept.
overclocked_otter writes "Intellon today announced the Pentode III at 600MHz, featuring a new 0.18 micron process and an on-die cache that actually runs at full speed. The chip lists at $669 in quantities of a thousand, which means about $1,100 at the store down the street. Benchmarks show it beating last month's chip by a margin noticeable only to benchmarks." Meanwhile Advanced Micro Gizmos says its Marathon will hit 700 before Christmas. Your move, Intellon.

The new core adds what Intellon calls Streaming SIMD Extensions Plus, which according to the press release "enhances the Internet experience." How exactly a floating point instruction set makes web pages arrive faster over a 56k modem is left as an exercise for the reader.
