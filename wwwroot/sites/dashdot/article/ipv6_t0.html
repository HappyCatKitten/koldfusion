IPv6 Test Network Hits 400 Sites
Posted by Rotundo on Tuesday October 12, @07:10PM EDT
from the enough-addresses-for-every-grain-of-sand dept.
HexHead writes "The experimental IPv6 backbone now connects more than 400 sites across 40 countries. With 128-bit addresses there will be enough to give an IP address to every grain of sand on every beach, which should help with the impending sand-networking boom." Adoption is expected any day now, give or take a decade.
