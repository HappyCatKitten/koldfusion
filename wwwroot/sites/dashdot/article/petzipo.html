Petz.com IPO Soars Despite Losing Money on Every Sale
Posted by CmdrGrits on Saturday October 16, @01:00PM EDT
from the we'll-make-it-up-in-volume dept.
DayTraderDan writes "Online pet store Petz.com, whose sock puppet mascot has become oddly famous, went public today and closed up 85%. According to the S-1, the company sells 40-pound bags of dog food for less than the cost of shipping them. When asked about the business model, a spokesperson said: 'Eyeballs.'" I'm not saying it's a bubble. I'm saying I just saw a sock puppet with a market cap larger than my town.
