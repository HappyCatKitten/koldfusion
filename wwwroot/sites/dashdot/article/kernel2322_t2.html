Linix Kernel 2.3.22 Released; 2.4 'Real Soon Now'
Posted by Hemlock on Sunday October 17, @11:05PM EDT
from the development-kernels-are-for-developers dept.
kernel_kid writes "The latest development kernel is out, with USB updates, a reworked VM, and a new filesystem driver for reading disks formatted by a 1987 word processor. As always, this is a development series. It will eat your disk, your cat and your homework. 2.4 is expected 'when it is done,' which is the official release date of all good software." Download it from your nearest mirror. Please, the nearest. The main site is hosted on a 486 in a closet.
