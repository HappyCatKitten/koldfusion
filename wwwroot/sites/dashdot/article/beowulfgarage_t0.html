Teen Builds 64-Node Cluster in Parents' Garage
Posted by Rotundo on Friday October 15, @06:22PM EDT
from the imagine-a-beowulf-cluster-of-these dept.
NodeNinja writes "A 16-year-old in Ohio has built a 64-node cluster out of discarded 486s and Pentium 75s collected from local businesses upgrading for Y2K. It runs Linix and PVM, heats the garage through the winter, and according to the builder's father, 'has made the electric meter spin like a ceiling fan.'" Finally, a story where the joke has already been made for you in the department line.
