Ostrich Satellite Phone Network to Deorbit 66 Satellites
Posted by Pixelfish on Friday October 15, @09:05AM EDT
from the can-you-hear-me-now dept.
LowEarthLarry writes "After its bankruptcy, the Ostrich satellite phone network has announced it will steer all 66 of its satellites into the atmosphere to burn up. The handsets were the size of a brick, cost $3,000, and only worked outdoors, which turned out to be a hard sell to business travelers who spend most of their time indoors." Five billion dollars of hardware turned into the world's most expensive meteor shower.
