Your Rights Online: Project Earwig: Are Your E-Mails Being Read?
Posted by jamie_k on Wednesday October 13, @09:12PM EDT
from the hi-to-everyone-reading-this dept.
ParanoidPolly writes "A European parliamentary report has described Project Earwig, an alleged international network of listening stations that sifts phone calls, faxes and e-mail for keywords. A group of netizens has declared October 21 'Jam Earwig Day' and is asking everyone to put suspicious-sounding words in their e-mail signatures." Hello to all our readers in the listening stations. Please moderate this story up.
