Ask Dashdot: How Do I Explain My Job to My Grandmother?
Posted by Rotundo on Sunday October 17, @05:40PM EDT
from the it's-like-a-typewriter-but dept.
sysadmin_steve writes "I'm a Unix system administrator. Every Thanksgiving my grandmother asks what I do, and every year I say 'I keep the computers running' and she asks if I can fix her VCR clock. This year I want to do better. How do you explain sysadmin work, network engineering, or (heaven help you) database administration to someone who still writes checks at the supermarket?" My personal approach: 'I'm a plumber, but the pipes are invisible and the water is made of numbers.'
