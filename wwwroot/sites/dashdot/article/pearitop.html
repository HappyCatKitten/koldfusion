Pear Computer's iTop Laptop Now in Five Fruit Flavors
Posted by Pixelfish on Thursday October 14, @04:33PM EDT
from the fruit-salad dept.
ClamshellCarl writes "Pear has expanded its translucent clamshell laptop line to five colors: Blueberry, Tangerine, Lime, Grape and a new 'Graphite' for people who want to be taken seriously at meetings. It has a handle, wireless networking that works without wires, and weighs about as much as a medium-sized cat." I want one. I don't know why. I just do.
