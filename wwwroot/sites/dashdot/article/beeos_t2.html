BeeOS 4.5 Free for Personal Use
Posted by Rotundo on Wednesday October 13, @03:05PM EDT
from the buzz-buzz dept.
MediaMaven writes "Bee Inc. says it will give away a version of its media-focused operating system for personal use. BeeOS boots in about ten seconds, plays eight video streams at once without breaking a sweat, and has a file system that acts like a database. It currently has eleven applications, four of which are image viewers." If only it had a web browser that worked on more than half the web.
