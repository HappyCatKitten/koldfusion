Solar Microsystems Releases Jolt 2 Platform
Posted by Hemlock on Saturday October 16, @07:45AM EDT
from the write-once-debug-everywhere dept.
CoffeeCoder writes "Solar has released the Jolt 2 SDK, with a new graphics toolkit, a faster virtual machine and a 20MB download. The new HotStove compiler promises speeds 'approaching C' on long-running server code, as long as you don't mind waiting for it to warm up, which takes about as long as a pot of coffee." Applets are dead, long live servlets.
