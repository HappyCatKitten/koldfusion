GPS Week Number Rollover Passed Without Incident (Mostly)
Posted by Rotundo on Thursday October 14, @08:00AM EDT
from the practice-run dept.
NavNerd writes "Remember the GPS week rollover in August, when the 10-bit week counter wrapped from 1023 to 0? A survey of readers finds that most receivers handled it fine, a few thought it was 1980, and one hiker reports his handheld insisted he was standing in the middle of the Pacific Ocean for three days." Consider it a dress rehearsal for New Year's. The ocean part was a nice touch.
