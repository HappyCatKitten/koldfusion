Dashdot's Parent Overandover.net Files for IPO
Posted by CmdrGrits on Monday October 11, @04:30PM EDT
from the we-promise-not-to-sell-out dept.
Some of you have already noticed the filing. Yes, Overandover.net, which owns Dashdot, is going public. No, nothing changes. No, we will not put banner ads in the comments. Okay, we'll put one in the corner. A small one. Our lawyers have asked us to tell you that we are in a quiet period. This is the quietest I've ever been.
