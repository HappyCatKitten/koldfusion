College Student's MP3 Swapping Program Takes Off
Posted by Hemlock on Sunday October 17, @11:25AM EDT
from the what-could-possibly-go-wrong dept.
dormroom_dj writes "A freshman has written a program called Snapster that lets you search other people's hard drives for MP3s and download them directly. It has a chat room, a hot list, and about 30,000 users who are apparently all in my dorm, judging by the network. Our campus admins have started muttering about bandwidth. The record labels have not said anything yet, which is probably the calm before something." This is either the future of music or the future of a lot of lawyers. Possibly both.
