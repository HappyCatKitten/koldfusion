Yowza! Help

What is Yowza!?
Yowza! is a guide to the World Wide Web, organized by real people into categories you can browse, like the shelves of a library that never closes and never shushes you. Each listing was visited and described by one of our surfers.

What do the symbols mean?
means our surfers think the site is especially cool.
means the site or category was added recently.
A category name ending in @ lives somewhere else in the directory; clicking it takes you there.
The number in parentheses, like (12), is how many sites are filed in that category and everything below it.

How do I search?
Type a few words in the box and press Search. Yowza! looks for categories and sites whose names and descriptions contain every word you typed. From any category page you can choose to search just this category. If Yowza! comes up empty, the search results page will offer to pass your words along to AltaVisor, which reads the full text of Web pages.

How do I get my site listed?
Go to the category where it belongs and click Suggest a Site at the bottom of the page.

Why is my site not listed yet?
Our surfers are reading as fast as they can. Bribes in the form of baked goods are not accepted, but they are eaten.

What browser do I need?
Any browser from 1996 or later. Search, My Yowza! and the Random Link use JavaScript, so please leave it on.
