More Yowza!

Yowza! is more than a directory. Here is everything else we do, or are about to do, or have announced at a trade show.
  • My Yowza! - your personal start page
  • Yowza! Mail - free email for life
  • Auctions - our friends at uBid
  • GeoHoods - free home pages
  • News - headlines from CNM Interactive
  • Yellow Pages - Find a plumber, a pizza place or a guy who fixes modems, by city and category.
  • People Search - Look up long-lost friends by name, city or high school mascot.
  • Maps - Type an address and get a map with a little red star on it. Driving directions coming soon.
  • Travel - Book flights, hotels and rental cars. Our travel agents work from home in their pajamas.
  • Classifieds - Buy and sell used cars, couches and lava lamps in your neighborhood.
  • Personals - Single web surfer, likes long walks to the computer. Meet someone who also types in lowercase.
  • Games - Play hearts, checkers, chess and tic-tac-toe against people on the other side of the planet.
  • Chat - Talk with other Yowza! users in real time. Please do not ask everyone their age, sex and location.
  • Clubs - Start a club about anything: knitting, Linux, knitting with Linux. Free message boards and photo albums.
  • Calendar - Keep track of birthdays, meetings and the end of the millennium. December 31 is already marked.
  • Pager - See when your buddies are online and send them instant messages. Beep!
  • Sports - Scores, standings and the latest trade rumors, updated every few minutes.
  • Weather - Forecasts for 10,000 cities. Today: partly cloudy with a chance of Y2K.
  • TV - What is on tonight, on all 57 channels.
  • Stock Quotes - Delayed quotes for every stock, including the 40 internet companies that went public this morning.
  • Autos - New and used car prices, reviews and a minivan for every family.
  • Careers - Thousands of jobs, many of which require knowing HTML.
  • Greetings - Send a free animated greeting card with a MIDI soundtrack. Your friends will love it (once).
  • Health - Health news and a medical encyclopedia written in plain English.
  • Net Events - Live chats with celebrities, webcasts and online concerts. Times are Pacific, sorry.
  • Message Boards - Thousands of discussion boards on every topic. Arguments start at the third post.
  • Movies - Showtimes, reviews and trailers (if you have a very fast modem).
  • Music - Charts, news and where to buy the CD.
  • Real Estate - Find a house, an apartment or a bunker with three years of canned goods.
  • Small Business - Start a business, get a web site, and incorporate before lunch.
  • Yowzlings! - A directory just for kids, with only the safe and the silly.
